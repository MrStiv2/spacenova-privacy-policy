Spacenova: Storage Cleaner
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy describes how Spacenova: Storage Cleaner (also known by its development name CleanItUp), published by Serhii Fedorenko / CampStudio, handles information. Contact: campstudiomail@gmail.com.

1. Your photos, videos and files

Gallery scanning, duplicate and similarity analysis, video compression, file review and cleanup operate on your device. Spacenova does not upload your media library, file contents, gallery-analysis database or private-vault contents to a CampStudio server. Access is limited by the permissions and documents or folders you provide. Scanning does not delete media. Deletion requires your selection and confirmation; compression saves a new copy before any separate choice to remove the original.

System services remain separate from Spacenova. For example, Apple Photos and iCloud Photos may synchronize changes, and requesting an item not stored locally may cause the operating system to download it from your cloud library. Sharing or exporting a file sends it only to the destination you choose.

2. The private vault

Vault items are encrypted locally with AES-256-GCM and protected by a PIN you create and, optionally, your device's biometric authentication. Spacenova does not receive your fingerprint or face data; the operating system performs biometric checks. There is no CampStudio cloud vault backup. Export items you wish to keep before deleting the app or its data. Originals remain in your gallery unless you separately choose to delete them.

3. Advertising and third-party SDKs

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform for banners, optional rewarded advertising and consent. Advertising requires a network connection. These services may transmit information off your device, including:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it;
  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier when permitted;
  • advertising information, such as ads viewed and ad interactions;
  • product interactions, such as app launches or taps associated with advertising;
  • crash reports, performance information and other technical diagnostics.

Google and its partners may use this information for advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, security, fraud prevention and improving their services. Device identifiers can be used for tracking across other companies' apps and websites where you have granted the required permission. Advertising data, interactions and approximate location may be associated with device identifiers. Spacenova requests non-personalized ads and disables Google's publisher first-party identifier and publisher personalization; these settings do not mean that all advertising or diagnostic data collection stops.

See Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner services, and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures. Third-party processing may take place in countries other than your own.

4. Consent and choices

Where applicable, advertising consent is requested through Google UMP. On iOS the app requests permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before permitted advertising tracking. Declining tracking does not block media review or purchases. You can change tracking and photo-access permissions in system settings and access advertising privacy options in the app when the consent SDK makes them available. Premium removes banners; rewarded advertising is optional.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions and operation packs are processed by Apple App Store / StoreKit on iOS and Google Play on Android. Spacenova uses purchase and entitlement information to grant Premium access or operation credits and restore eligible purchases. CampStudio does not receive payment-card or bank-account details. These stores handle billing and subscription management under their own policies. No Spacenova account or sign-in is required.

6. Local settings, reminders and retention

Preferences, quotas, cleanup history and analysis caches are stored locally. Optional reminders use operating-system notifications and can be disabled. Vault keys and other sensitive local values use platform secure storage. The app has no separate cloud account or account-deletion process. Use available app controls to clear accessible caches and export or remove vault files. Removing app data or uninstalling may remove local files and settings, but does not necessarily erase operating-system secure storage, device backups or information retained independently by advertising or store providers.

7. Contact and privacy requests

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include in your message and use it to respond. Do not send private photos, vault credentials or payment information unless specifically necessary for a support request. Contact campstudiomail@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests concerning information held by CampStudio. For data held by Apple, Google or other providers, use their privacy and account controls.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when app functionality or service providers change. The revision date above identifies the current version. This policy applies to Spacenova; policies for other CampStudio products are separate.
