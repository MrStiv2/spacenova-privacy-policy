Spacenova review demonstrations

Recorded on a separate Android emulator with local synthetic data. This is the QA variant of the submitted 2012 source; Premium is simulated, with no real purchase. Production UI and service implementations are unchanged. Videos are edited only to remove waiting.

Installed-app inventory

QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES enables the on-device list of installed packages. Android usage access separately provides measured app/data/cache sizes. This inventory is not sent to advertising or analytics services.

Accessibility disclosure and consent

The optional cache assistant presents an in-app explanation before opening Android Accessibility settings. It operates only on the user's explicit cache-cleaning request, never clears app data or uninstalls apps, and does not store or transmit screen content.

Background video compression

The user chooses videos and compression quality and starts a local compression job. A foreground-service notification remains visible while the app is backgrounded, with an explicit Cancel action. Originals remain unchanged.
